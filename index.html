The Director.

Born from a collision of two worlds. French precision and Vietnamese instinct. A filmmaker who learned to see in Paris and learned to feel in Saigon.

Every project begins with an obsession: light as it breaks, a face before it knows it is being watched, the silence between two cuts that changes everything. Commercial or personal, the question is always the same: what does this moment actually look like?

Based in Ho Chi Minh City. Working across Vietnam and beyond. Commercials, short films, fashion films, documentary.
